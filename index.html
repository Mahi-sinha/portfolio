Projects

Three I'd actually want to walk you through in an interview.

01

Vi-Notes

Academic integrity detection

The problem: instructors have no easy way to tell whether a submitted piece of writing was actually typed by the student or pasted in from somewhere else, AI included.

What I built: as part of a team during the IIT Ropar NPTEL internship, I worked on a tool that tracks behavioural signals during writing — paste count, words-per-minute — and turns them into an authenticity score per submission. Session history is stored so instructors can review and flag suspicious submissions later rather than only at the moment of submission.

Stack: React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, TypeScript

02

Cyberbullying Detection System

NLP classifier

The problem: moderating chat or message content for hate speech and bullying at any scale means you can't rely on manual review alone.

What I built: an NLP-based classifier that takes a message or chat excerpt and predicts whether it constitutes hate speech or cyberbullying. I handled text preprocessing and trained and evaluated the model against labelled datasets, iterating on preprocessing choices to improve classification quality.

Stack: Python, NLP, Machine Learning
